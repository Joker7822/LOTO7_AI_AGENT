# Signal Expert Attribution Future-OOS

## Purpose

This protocol measures which existing Champion signal experts contribute to the locked Champion probability ranking on genuinely future draws. It is Research-only and has no Production, ticket, promotion, or model-selection authority.

The observation horizon is 26 trusted future draws, starting at the first unresolved target available when the protocol is initialized. Interim attribution results must not be used to remove experts, change Hedge weights, change the locked Champion config, or otherwise alter this shadow during the horizon.

## Locked signal model

The protocol locks the Champion config at protocol start and reconstructs its online Hedge state from prefix-only history. For each future target it freezes, before the result:

- the ordered expert keys,
- pre-result Hedge log weights,
- effective expert weights after `expert_uniform_mix`,
- all 37 probabilities for every expert,
- the final 37-number Champion q vector,
- the exact per-expert contribution to `final_q - Uniform`,
- all leave-one-expert-out q vectors,
- hashes for all prefrozen vectors,
- the current Top-7 and per-number expert contribution table.

The current expert family is generated by `expert_probabilities()` and currently contains 11 experts: hot 20/50/100/200, EWMA 10/30/60, overdue, recent_cold, momentum, and pair_context.

## Exact additive attribution

Let `u = 1/37`, `w_i` be the prefrozen effective Hedge weight for expert `i`, `q_i` its 37-number distribution, and `f` the locked final Uniform mix. The Champion score is

`q = f*u + (1-f) * sum_i w_i q_i`.

Therefore

`q - u = sum_i (1-f) * w_i * (q_i - u)`.

The protocol freezes each term on the right before the result. This gives an exact additive attribution of every number's score deviation from Uniform.

After the trusted result is known, the same identity gives exact additive actual-mass attribution:

`mass(q, actual) - 7/37 = sum_i sum_{n in actual} contribution_i[n]`.

The implementation fails closed if this identity does not hold numerically.

## Log and Brier diagnostics

Log score and Brier score are nonlinear, so they are not presented as additive expert contributions. Instead the protocol freezes a leave-one-expert-out counterfactual for every expert by renormalizing the already-prefrozen effective weights over the remaining experts and applying the same locked final Uniform mix.

After the result, it records the change between the full Champion and each prefrozen leave-one-out distribution for:

- Top-7 hits,
- actual probability mass,
- mean log probability of the 7 winning numbers,
- Brier score,
- Top-7 membership displacement.

These are descriptive counterfactual diagnostics, not causal estimates and not independent hypothesis tests.

## Trust and fail-closed rules

A result is graded only when the existing source validation reports `verified_two_result_sources` for the exact latest round.

The protocol fails closed when:

- a target result is already passed without a prefrozen record,
- any stored vector hash is inconsistent,
- per-expert contributions do not reconstruct the final Champion q,
- exact actual-mass contributions do not sum to the full Champion mass edge vs Uniform,
- a same-target Champion calibration shadow exists but its prefrozen base-q hash disagrees.

A missing reference is never reconstructed after its result is known.

## Interpretation boundary

This protocol is for diagnosis of the existing signal stack. It does not establish lottery predictability, does not authorize Production changes, and must not be used for interim expert pruning or weight tuning within the 26-draw horizon. Any future expert redesign should be evaluated in a separate Research protocol and, if appropriate, registered as a new prospective challenger.
